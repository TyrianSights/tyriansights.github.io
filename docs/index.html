02 / THE PLANNED ECOSYSTEM

Connected by intention.

A family of wearable and ambient tools,
designed to work together around you.

Our long-term vision brings sight, touch, and motion together. These planned products explore how personal technology can support productivity, creativity, and presence.

Flagship eyewear concept

TS-1 Glasses

Smart glasses with a signature bullseye display, exploring augmented-reality overlays, navigation, and focus assistance. Touch, voice, and gesture interaction are part of the longer-term vision.

Vision · Focus · Augmented experience

Smart wristband concept

SightsBand

A discreet wristband exploring haptic reminders, gesture controls, and coordination with the TS-1. The proposed design pairs a matte-black finish with a Tyrian-purple accent.

Touch · Movement · Subtle cues

Pocket assistant concept

FocusTile

A portable digital anchor for focus sessions, reminders, and time blocks. The concept combines a minimal square form with touch, voice, and haptic interaction.

Smart ring concept

Tyrian Ring

A minimal ring exploring tap commands, subtle gestures, NFC access, and quiet alerts. The aim is to make small, intentional interactions feel natural.

THE SOFTWARE VISION

Tyrian Vision OS

A proposed shared platform built around modularity, privacy-first AI, and cross-device coordination. The vision includes customizable focus modes, spatial interfaces, and future tools for developers. This platform and its integrations are still concepts.

03 / MISSION & VISION

Focus.
Interaction.
Expression.

Our mission

Tyrian Sights empowers individuals to engage with technology through intuitive, wearable interfaces that elevate focus, interaction, and expression.

We merge tactile innovation with minimalist design, working toward a modular ecosystem of smart accessories that integrates into daily life and enhances productivity, creativity, and personal presence.

Our vision

To lead the future of personal technology by creating a connected world where sight, touch, and motion converge into intricately designed, intelligent tools.

We envision glasses, a ring, a tile, and a band working together: adapting to you, responding to your environment, and opening new possibilities for insight and interaction.

04 / WHAT GUIDES US

People at the center.

Seven principles shape what we design
and the kind of company we want to build.

01Design With Dignity

Technology must respect and uplift people. Privacy, agency, and accessibility guide our designs, protecting people’s rights and honoring individuality.

02Inclusive By Default

We aim to build adaptive tools for people across gender, race, neurodiversity, language, ability, religion, nationality, body type, sexual orientation, and economic background.

03Sustainability Through Simplicity

Fewer materials, modular systems, and long-term function over planned obsolescence. Repairability, upgradeability, and reuse are design goals from the beginning.

04Innovation Without Elitism

The future is co-created. We want builders, artists, developers, and educators to help shape our tools and share in their progress.

05Empathy-Driven Tech

Technology should respond to how people feel, move, and communicate. We explore touch, motion, and focus as ways to enhance everyday experiences quietly.

06Lead With Purpose

We aim to advance personal technology with integrity, responsibility, and care for our communities. Ethical leadership means thinking about the long term.

07Celebrate Diversity in Design

More perspectives make better products. We value multicultural, multilingual communities and want those perspectives represented throughout the design process.

Respect, integrity, continuous learning, and a healthy work/life balance belong in our working culture. Sustainability includes both the resources we use and the wellbeing of the people doing the work.

05 / THE NAME & THE IDEA

See with intention.
Act with precision.

Tyrian takes its name from Tyrian purple, a historic color of rare value. Sights stands for vision and perspective: clarity, ambition, and the ability to imagine what comes next.

Our original emblem concept pairs purple glasses with a bullseye in one lens. The glasses represent insight; the bullseye represents deliberate attention; the crosshairs represent precision and alignment.

The design language combines Tyrian Purple, Midnight Black, Opalescent White, and Neon Aqua with four pillars: precision, elegance, augmentation, and integration.

Our maker roots are in imaginative gadgets, wearable electronics, and tactile objects. We aim to grow through experimentation, careful testing, and continuous learning.
