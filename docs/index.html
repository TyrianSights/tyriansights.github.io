01 / ABOUT TYRIAN SIGHTS

Our mission and vision

Personal technology designed around people.

Our mission

Empower people through intuitive, wearable interfaces that elevate focus, interaction, and expression.

We combine tactile innovation with minimalist design to support productivity, creativity, and personal presence.

Our vision

A connected world where sight, touch, and motion work together through thoughtful, intelligent tools.

We envision glasses, a ring, a tile, and a band adapting to you and working together throughout your day.

02 / WHAT GUIDES US

Our seven values

Seven principles shape what we design
and the kind of company we want to build.

01

Design With Dignity

Technology must respect and uplift people. Privacy, agency, and accessibility guide our designs, protecting people’s rights and honoring individuality.

02

Inclusive By Default

We aim to build adaptive tools for people across gender, race, neurodiversity, language, ability, religion, nationality, body type, sexual orientation, and economic background.

03

Sustainability Through Simplicity

Fewer materials, modular systems, and long-term function over planned obsolescence. Repairability, upgradeability, and reuse are design goals from the beginning.

04

Innovation Without Elitism

The future is co-created. We want builders, artists, developers, and educators to help shape our tools and share in their progress.

05

Empathy-Driven Tech

Technology should respond to how people feel, move, and communicate. We explore touch, motion, and focus as ways to enhance everyday experiences quietly.

06

Lead With Purpose

We aim to advance personal technology with integrity, responsibility, and care for our communities. Ethical leadership means thinking about the long term.

07

Celebrate Diversity in Design

More perspectives make better products. We value multicultural, multilingual communities and want those perspectives represented throughout the design process.

Respect, integrity, continuous learning, and a healthy work/life balance belong in our working culture. Sustainability includes both the resources we use and the wellbeing of the people doing the work.

06 / THE NAME & THE IDEA

The story behind Tyrian Sights

Tyrian takes its name from Tyrian purple, a historic color of rare value. Sights stands for vision and perspective: clarity, ambition, and the ability to imagine what comes next.

Our original emblem concept pairs purple glasses with a bullseye in one lens. The glasses represent insight; the bullseye represents deliberate attention; the crosshairs represent precision and alignment.

The design language combines Tyrian Purple, Midnight Black, Opalescent White, and Neon Aqua with four pillars: precision, elegance, augmentation, and integration.

Our maker roots are in imaginative gadgets, wearable electronics, and tactile objects. We aim to grow through experimentation, careful testing, and continuous learning.
